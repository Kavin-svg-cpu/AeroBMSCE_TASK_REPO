Who we are

Engineers from 10+ departments, one flight line.

AeroBMSCE is the official aerospace, aeromodelling and unmanned systems innovation club of BMS College of Engineering, operating from Propel Lab–3 since 2012.

About the club

Design. Build. Test. Fly.

Established in 2012, the club brings together engineers from more than ten departments to design, build and test advanced aircraft, drones and autonomous systems.

Since we started, we’ve taken on and completed 75+ projects and produced 50+ research papers, reports and certifications that highlight the creativity and dedication of our members.

What we do

  • Competitive aircraft & UAV design
  • Drone racing & autonomy
  • Industry-grade fabrication & testing
  • Talent development through real-world engineering
